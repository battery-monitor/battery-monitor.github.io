Privacy policy

This policy covers battery and power readings on your Mac, local preferences, support email and Apple purchase services.

Privacy contact

Contact Zhiping Jiang with a privacy question or a request to delete support correspondence.

jiangzp.cs@icloud.com

Battery and power readings

BatterVibe reads macOS battery, power and temperature information locally. Live readings are held in memory. The app keeps no battery history database and sends no readings or hardware serial numbers to the developer.

Processor activity and short-term history

While BatterVibe is running and the Mac is awake, per-core activity is sampled locally and only the last 20 seconds are kept in memory. Sampling continues with the panel closed. Sleep pauses sampling and clears the history. The standalone edition also reads supported GPU, chip power, and recent app energy data locally. Peak test results remain until the next run or until you quit.

Local preferences and permissions

Menu bar display, language and launch-at-login choices stay on the Mac. macOS may request approval for the background service used by closed-lid and power controls. The app requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics.

Storage and deletion

Quitting releases live readings. Preferences remain until you remove the corresponding app data using macOS. The developer cannot access or delete this local data remotely.

Trials and purchases

Store version 1.1 uses Apple StoreKit for trials, one-time purchases and restoration. The app reads the transaction status needed to determine access. Apple handles payment information and purchase records.

Support email and website visits

If you contact support, we use your email address and message to respond and investigate. You may request deletion, subject to legal retention requirements. The website has no third-party analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process routine logs for operation and security. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store services.
