Privacy policy

Battery Monitor does not collect or transmit battery or power readings.

Privacy contact

Contact Zhiping Jiang with privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.

jiangzp.cs@icloud.com

On-device readings

Battery Monitor does not collect or transmit battery or power readings. It reads information provided by macOS and displays it locally. Live readings are processed in memory, with no battery history database. Hardware serial numbers are not retained in the display model or sent to the developer.

Preferences and permissions

Display preferences and first-run state are stored locally on your Mac. The app does not require an account and includes no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or telemetry. It does not read personal documents, contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone data for its features. Opening a support or privacy link uses your system browser.

Storage and deletion

Live readings are released when the app exits. Local preferences remain until the app's data is removed using macOS facilities. The developer has no remote copy of, or access to, your local app data.

Support requests

If you voluntarily email support, your email address and message are used only to respond and investigate your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence, except information that must be retained by law. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Website and Apple services

This website embeds no advertising or third-party analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary access logs to serve and secure the site. App Store downloads, updates, and Apple's own data handling are governed by Apple's policies.

Contact and updates

Contact jiangzp.cs@icloud.com for privacy questions or support correspondence deletion. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
