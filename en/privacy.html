Privacy policy

BatterVibe processes battery and power readings locally without collecting or uploading them. This policy also describes the Apple services planned for the purchase features in development.

Privacy contact

Contact Zhiping Jiang with privacy questions or to request deletion of support correspondence.

jiangzp.cs@icloud.com

On-device readings

BatterVibe reads information provided by macOS and displays it locally without collecting or transmitting battery or power readings. Live readings are processed in memory, with no battery history database. Hardware serial numbers are not retained in the display model or sent to the developer.

Preferences and permissions

Display and language preferences and first-run state are stored locally on your Mac. The app requires no account and includes no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or telemetry. It does not read personal documents, contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone data for its features. Support and privacy links open in your system browser.

Storage and deletion

Live readings are released when the app exits. Local preferences remain until the app’s data is removed using macOS facilities. The developer has no remote copy of, or access to, your local app data.

Support requests

If you voluntarily email support, your email address and message are used only to respond and investigate your request. You may request deletion of support correspondence, except information that must be retained by law. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Trial and purchases (in development)

The planned trial, one-time purchase and purchase restoration use Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payments and purchase records; the app reads the required entitlement status. The developer does not receive payment card details. A purchase or trial does not create a battery history database or require a separate BatterVibe account.

Website and Apple services

This website embeds no advertising or third-party analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary access logs to serve and secure the site. App Store downloads, updates, and Apple’s own data handling are governed by Apple’s policies.

Contact and updates

Contact jiangzp.cs@icloud.com for privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence. Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
