A Mac menu bar app for Vibe Coding

When your Mac slows down, see which cores are busy.

Check processor activity, battery life, and power in your menu bar during a long build, a local model run, or a session on portable power. See whether your charger is keeping up while you work.

See 20 seconds of work, core by core.

Overview starts with current activity percentages. Hover to open the heatmap or click to keep it open. Each CPU core has its own row. Deeper color means the core was busy for more of that second.

On Apple silicon, the standalone edition groups performance and efficiency cores and shows overall GPU activity. Select a CPU row for its activity and the clock of its cluster, or the GPU row for an estimated clock. Available wattage appears below.

Battery details

Find out if your charger is keeping up.

Compare input power, system draw, and net battery power in Overview. A negative net reading means the battery is supplying the difference. When readings are available, see time to full or estimated battery life.

Open Battery Details to compare full charge and design capacity and check health, cycles, and temperature. Supported adapters also show power, voltage, and current.

Desktop controls

Keep your screen readable and your work running.

The standalone Overview puts brightness, closed-lid operation, charging, and power mode within reach.

Outdoor brightness
Turn on HDR boost to use the extra brightness of a compatible built-in XDR display. Switch it off indoors to conserve power.
Run with Lid Closed
Start your build or download, then turn on Run with Lid Closed. The background service keeps it running. Turning the switch off restores the previous sleep settings.
Charging and power mode
Expand Charge limit to choose a target. A manual power mode selection applies to both battery and external power.

Peak Power Test

Measure peak CPU and GPU power.

Start Peak Power Test from the gear menu to load the CPU and GPU together for up to 60 seconds. Readings update twice per second, and the window retains each chip’s peak.

Available in the standalone edition on supported Apple silicon Macs. You can stop early. Results remain until the next run or until you quit.

Read power from Terminal

Read battery and power data as JSON for your scripts, or change the menu bar reading from Terminal.
