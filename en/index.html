Battery details

How much charge can your battery hold today?

Compare full charge capacity with design capacity, then check battery health, cycle count and temperature. When you plug in, the Battery tab also shows the negotiated power limit, input voltage and current.

For a time estimate, return to Overview. It shows time to full while charging and estimates remaining runtime while discharging, even when plugged in, when enough data is available.

Desktop controls

Code in daylight. Let a build run with the lid closed.

Overview brings together the desktop controls available on your Mac in the standalone app.

Outdoor brightness
Turn on HDR boost to use the extra brightness of a compatible built-in XDR display. Switch it off indoors to conserve power.
Run with Lid Closed
Start your build or download, then turn on Run with Lid Closed. The background service keeps it running. Turning the switch off restores the previous sleep settings.
Charging and power mode
Expand Charge limit to choose a target. A manual power mode selection applies to both battery and external power.

Peak Power Test

Measure peak CPU and GPU power separately.

Open Peak Power Test from the gear menu. It loads the CPU and GPU together for up to 60 seconds, samples power every half second and keeps the highest reading for each chip component.

Available in the standalone app on Apple silicon Macs that report chip power. You can stop early. Peaks stay in the window until the next run or until you quit the app.

Read power from Terminal

Pass battery status, power and time estimates to a script as JSON, or change what the menu bar displays from Terminal.

Command-line guide →
battervibe status --json
battervibe menu net
