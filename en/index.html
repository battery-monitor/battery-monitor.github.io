Stay in your editor

Know how your Mac is using power.

See battery level, input power, system use, and net battery power. The overview shows available charge and runtime estimates. Open Battery Details for battery health, cycles, temperature, and more.

Input power is the DC reading at the Mac’s port. Available readings depend on the Mac, macOS, and power state.

Make room for nature

More daylight. More clarity.

Find a fresh view and keep making things. Use a compatible display’s extra brightness to read code and preview your website in natural light.

HDR brightness boost
Use the extra brightness headroom of a compatible XDR display for viewing in bright surroundings.
On when you need it
Turn on HDR boost when you need extra brightness, then turn it off when you head indoors.

HDR has been verified on the built-in XDR display of MacBook Pro (Mac14,6); other models await verification. Boosting brightness may use more power.

Leave the waiting to your Mac

The build is still running. The download is not done. Let your Mac keep working on the desk, ready for you to pick up where you left off.

Charge limit

Set a target on a compatible Apple silicon Mac running macOS 26.4 or later. The App Store update uses an Apple shortcut.

In-app purchase

Try advanced controls free for 14 days or unlock them with a one-time in-app purchase.

Restore purchases

Sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy and choose Restore Purchases in the control panel.
