01 / Stay in your editor

What is this build using?

AI coding, browser previews and build tests all draw power. Keep input, system usage or net battery power in the menu bar, and see how your Mac is doing without leaving your work.

Readings vary by device, macOS and power state. These are Mac-wide measurements, not power attributed to individual apps.

02 / Make room for nature

More daylight. More clarity.

Find a fresh view and keep making things. Use a compatible display’s extra brightness to read code and preview your website in natural light.

HDR brightness boost
Use the extra brightness headroom of a compatible XDR display for viewing in bright surroundings.
On when you need it
Turn on HDR boost when you need extra brightness, then turn it off when you head indoors.

HDR has been verified on the built-in XDR display of MacBook Pro (Mac14,6); other models await verification. Boosting brightness may use more power.

03 / Leave the waiting to your Mac

Close the lid. Keep the flow.

The build is still running. The download is not done. Let your Mac keep working on the desk, ready for you to pick up where you left off.

Project buildsDownloadsLong-running agent tasks

Closed-lid operation requires the website installation’s background component and system authorization. Support through the Mac App Store is still being verified. Keep the Mac on a ventilated, stationary surface.
