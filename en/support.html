Read and control from Terminal

After installing website distribution 0.10.1 or the Mac App Store 1.1 update, open Terminal and use the appropriate command. On first use it opens the menu bar app.

Website distribution

alias battervibe='/Applications/battery-mon.app/Contents/MacOS/battery-mon cli'

Mac App Store 1.1 update

alias battervibe='/Applications/BatterVibe.app/Contents/MacOS/BatterVibe cli'

Try these commands

battervibe status --json
battervibe menu net
battervibe --help

HDR and charge limits follow the app’s device and access requirements. The App Store update uses the Apple shortcut for charging. Check the result in macOS Battery settings. The website distribution also provides closed-lid, power mode, and high-energy app commands.

Exit code 3 means the result is unconfirmed. Code 4 means the request was sent. Read the current state before issuing another change.

Where can I download BatterVibe?

Version 1.0 is available for free in the mainland China Mac App Store for battery and power monitoring. Version 1.1 is being prepared. You can install it from the App Store once it’s available.

How do the menu bar and control panel differ?

The menu bar provides a quick battery or power reading. The dedicated panel brings together feature explanations, purchase status and restoration, and a thank-you for your support. Individual features are operated through their respective in-app entry points.

Why is net battery power negative when plugged in?

Net battery power is input minus system usage. When your Mac uses more than the adapter supplies, the battery makes up the difference. Adapter wattage and live DC input are separate readings. A dash means that a reading is unavailable.

Can every Mac use the HDR brightness boost?

No. HDR boost depends on display capability. It has been verified on the built-in XDR display of MacBook Pro (Mac14,6); other models await verification. Unsupported devices are not promised a boost. Increased brightness may also use more power.

Which installation channels support closed-lid operation?

Closed-lid operation uses the website distribution’s background component and requires system approval. Turn it off in the menu bar when you finish.

How do I use the advanced controls?

When the Mac App Store 1.1 update is available, open Control Panel from the gear menu. Start a 14-day free trial or unlock the controls with a one-time CNY 6 in-app purchase. The trial ends on its own, and battery and power monitoring remain available.

How do I restore a purchase?

In the App Store update, sign in with the Apple Account you used to buy and choose Restore Purchases in the control panel.

Which languages are supported?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish. The website’s language menu keeps you on the corresponding page. App language preferences are stored on your Mac.

Can I set a charge limit?

On a compatible Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26.4 or later, choose 80%, 85%, 90%, 95%, or 100%. With the App Store update, add the supplied Apple shortcut first and verify the result in macOS Battery settings.

Which devices support basic monitoring?

Basic monitoring requires macOS 13 or later and is designed for MacBooks with a built-in battery. Readings vary by model and macOS. Desktop Macs have no battery-specific readings. HDR and closed-lid controls have additional device and installation requirements.
