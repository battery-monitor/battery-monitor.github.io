Where do I find the app after launch?

The panel opens on first launch. Click the battery icon in the menu bar to reopen it. Click again or press Esc to close it.

How do I change the menu bar reading?

Open the panel and use the gear menu at the bottom right. Under the menu bar display options, choose battery level, input, system usage, or net power.

Why is net power negative when plugged in?

Net power is input minus system usage. When your Mac uses more than its adapter supplies, the battery makes up the difference. The adapter’s rated wattage and the current DC input are separate readings.

What do ≈ and — mean?

An ≈ marks an estimate. A dash means that the reading is unavailable for the current Mac, macOS version, or power state.

How do I refresh, launch at login, or quit?

Click the refresh button at the bottom right to update the readings. Use the gear menu to turn on launch at login or quit the app. Launch at login is optional.

Which Macs are supported?

The Mac App Store edition requires macOS 13 or later and is designed for MacBooks with a built-in battery. Battery-specific readings are unavailable on desktop Macs.
