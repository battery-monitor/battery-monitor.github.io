Support

Understand BatterVibe’s release status, power readings, display controls, closed-lid support and purchase plans.

Can I download or try the new release now?

Not yet. The version currently submitted for review is the earlier battery monitor. BatterVibe’s display controls, dedicated control panel, Apple trial and one-time purchase are in development. The website will link to the new release when it is available.

How do the menu bar and control panel differ?

The menu bar provides a quick battery or power reading. The planned dedicated control panel shows fuller status, display and running controls, plus trial, purchase and restore options. They are parts of the same product.

Why is net battery power negative when plugged in?

Net battery power is input minus system usage. When your Mac uses more than the adapter supplies, the battery makes up the difference. Adapter wattage and live DC input are separate readings. A dash means that a reading is unavailable.

Can every Mac use the HDR brightness boost?

No. HDR boost depends on display capability. It has been verified on the built-in XDR display of MacBook Pro (Mac14,6); other models await verification. Unsupported devices are not promised a boost. Increased brightness may also use more power.

Which installation channels support closed-lid operation?

Closed-lid operation needs the website installation’s background component and system authorization. Ordinary sleep prevention in the Mac App Store channel does not by itself keep a closed Mac awake. Check the capabilities of the installed release and leave the Mac on a ventilated, stationary surface.

Will the trial charge me automatically?

No. The initial trial is free for 14 days, after which you choose whether to make a one-time Apple in-app purchase. The planned mainland China price is CNY 6, with no automatic renewal. This flow is in development; local prices will appear in Apple’s purchase screen after release.

How will I restore a purchase after reinstalling?

Restore Purchases is planned for the control panel. Once released, use the Apple account used for the original purchase to restore eligible entitlements. No separate BatterVibe account is planned. Purchases for the new release are not yet open.

Which languages are supported?

Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French and Spanish. The website’s language menu keeps you on the corresponding page. App language preferences are stored on your Mac.

Can I set a charge limit?

The upcoming release includes charge-limit control for Apple silicon Macs running macOS 26.4 or later. With Mac App Store installation, first add the official system shortcut as guided, then select a system-supported limit in the control panel. Starting the shortcut does not confirm a change; the limit must be read back from the system.

Which devices support basic monitoring?

Basic monitoring requires macOS 13 or later and is designed for MacBooks with a built-in battery. Readings vary by model and macOS. Desktop Macs have no battery-specific readings. HDR and closed-lid controls have additional device and installation requirements.
